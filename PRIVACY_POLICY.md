## Overview
CLNDR is an offline calendar, milestone tracking, and home screen widget application designed to be run locally on a user's Android device. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user data.

The short version: CLNDR does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data whatsoever.

## Information We Collect
We do not collect any information. Specifically:
- **No personal information:** The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.
- **No usage data:** The app does not track how you use it, how often you use it, or what features you interact with.
- **System Calendar Integration:** If you enable the calendar mirroring feature, CLNDR requests access to your device's system calendar to create, update, or delete milestone entries locally on your device. This data is handled entirely offline and is never transmitted to any third-party servers.
- **Notifications:** If you enable notifications, CLNDR uses local scheduling to trigger milestone reminders on your device. No notifications are sent or managed by remote servers.

## 1. Overview
CLNDR ("the Application") is a minimal, monochrome, widget-first Android calendar application developed by Surajit Das ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Application.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **Local Data Storage:** All calendar events, milestones, life grid states, and year progress configurations are stored **100% locally on your device** using Android's local Room database.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal identifiable information (PII).
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** The Application operates entirely offline and does not sync data to external servers or cloud services.
- **Sunrise/Sunset Formula:** Automated day/night theme transitions use an offline mathematical formula (NOAA simplified calculation) that does **not** collect or access your location or location permissions.

## 3. Device Permissions
The Application requests minimum system permissions required strictly for core functionality:
- **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Used to display notification reminders for user-created milestones.
- **`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`**: Used to trigger milestone notifications at the exact scheduled times set by the user.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics
- **No Third-Party SDKs:** The Application contains no third-party tracking, analytics (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics), crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
- **No Ads:** The Application is completely ad-free.

## 5. Security
Your data stays on your device. Security depends on your device-level security (PIN, passphrase, biometrics). Uninstalling the Application will remove all locally stored database entries.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Application does not address or collect data from anyone under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document and pushed to the repository.
